Challenge

Write a loop that prints the numbers:

10
9
8
7
6
5

Do not write six separate System.out.println() statements.

## Before moving on

You should be able to:

-Explain what a loop does
-Explain how a while loop works
-Identify the loop's condition
-Explain why an infinite loop can happen
-Use ++ and --
-Predict how many times a simple loop runs
-Understand why FTC uses while (opModeIsActive())
-Understand that conditionals can exist inside loops
